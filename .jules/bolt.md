## 2025-05-20 - Subtitle Line Matching Regex and Normalization Overhead
**Learning:** During TTS subtitle generation from cues (`_build_subtitle_items_from_edge_cues` and `_build_subtitle_items_from_legacy_submaker`), matching accumulated cue text against `script_lines` is called for every single cue event (hundreds to thousands of times per video). Re-compiling non-word regexes `re.sub(r"[_\W]+", ...)` and re-normalizing `script_lines[sub_index]` on every cue iteration incurs high CPU overhead. Pre-compiling `_NON_WORD_RE` at the module level and pre-normalizing `script_lines` into tuples `(line, line_stripped, norm, ar_norm)` prior to iterating cues yields a ~2.5x speedup.
**Action:** When performing iterative text matching or string accumulation loops over collections, pre-normalize target texts once outside the inner loop.
